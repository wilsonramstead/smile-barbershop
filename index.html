In the chair

Cuts that hold their shape.

Everything here is the standard barbershop menu done carefully — and the reviews keep coming back to the same word: detail.

Fades & tapers

Low, mid or high — blended clean through the sides and back so the growth-out stays even instead of falling apart in a week.

Line-ups & edge work

The part people drive across town for. Hairline, temples and neck squared off sharp, with the same edge on a fresh cut or a touch-up between them.

Beard trims & blends

Beard shaped and faded into the cut so the two read as one piece — cut and beard in the same visit is the most common request here.

Family & kids’ cuts

A family-oriented shop — the chairs see grandfathers, dads and first-graders on the same afternoon, and nobody rushes the little ones.

Why regulars stay

The line-up is the whole point.

Anyone can take length off. The difference shows up along the hairline — and it is the reason people here stop shopping around once they find the right chair.

Years, not visits. The loyalty in these reviews is measured in years with the same barber, not one lucky haircut.

Told, then advised. Reviewers describe a barber who listens to what you asked for first, then suggests what will actually suit you.

The whole floor can cut. Regulars ask for Alex by name, and they say the same thing about everybody else working the room.
